Six roles.
One talks to you.

Read the crew
  1. chat
    Your only interlocutorAnswers quick questions itself, with a scout when it must look.
    its replies
  2. crew_start
    Turns a task into a runRewrites your message as one complete brief.
    .harnes/runs/<id>/
  3. crew_status
    Follows every hand-offChecks each result was integrated before it reports.
    run-<id>
  1. os_brief
    Loads its methods firstproject, stepper, blueprint and execution packs.
    OS packs
  2. plan
    Splits the task into lots and wavesLots in one wave never share a file.
    plan.json
  3. threads
    Seeds one thread per lotThe lot's brief is its first message.
    lot-<id>-<lot>
  1. bar
    Sets the bar before any workCriteria, the evidence each needs, when to involve you.
    bar.json
  2. gate
    Checks the bar against the resultsSends the work back, or tells you it is ready.
    gate-<n>.json
  1. thread_read
    Reads its lot thread firstTouches only the files of its lot; asks in the thread.
    lot thread
  2. ctx_execute
    Builds and tests out of contextHeavy output is stored and searched, not read whole.
    harnes-ctx
  3. e2e_*
    Writes and runs its own e2e testObserve, click, fill, check, then save it for replay.
    reports/<lot>.json
  1. diff
    Reviews the diff against the planWith the real e2e results, not the reports' claims.
    review-<n>.json
  2. CHANGES
    Posts fixes in each lot threadFix workers pick them up as a new round.
    lot threads
  1. scout
    Short errands in parallelFind code, summarise files, run one command. Never edits.
    its reply
